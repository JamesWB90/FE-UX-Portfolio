About me

From music tech and project management to the web.

I came to coding later in life. After studying music technology and working in studios, on film sets and in retail, I spent six years as an office admin and project manager for a property developer — and helped set up and brand a local coffee shop. That's where I discovered UX design.

I then trained at the Bath Spa University web development bootcamp and have kept building ever since. Today I make complete websites for small businesses: designed with the user in mind, coded by hand, and backed by simple, secure servers.

Outside work you'll find me at the gym, on a bike, playing guitar, at motorsport track days in a car I built with my brothers — or keeping up with my two kids.
